The agency

About the agency.

Amsterdam booking for living Romani music and gypsy jazz.

Romani Music International Booking Agency is based in Amsterdam. We help venues, festivals, and private events find the right artists from our roster of living gypsy jazz and Romani music acts — matching the night, not just sending a list.

Send an inquiry to info@romanimusic.com. We reply ourselves, talk through the fit, then confirm the lineup and hold.

Write in Dutch, English, French, Italian, Spanish, German, Portuguese, or Chinese.

How we book

Small on purpose.

If a name is on the roster, you can inquire about it. If it is not, we will say so. We do not pad the list with artists we do not send out.

The four acts are Brady Winterstein Trio, Marcia Bamberg Swing Quintet, Daniel Gueli Gypsy Jazz Orchestra, and Django Rosenberg Trio. Bios on this site stay close to what is already public. Where a lineup or a touring note is not settled, the page says so.

Inquiries use the booking form. It opens an email to info@romanimusic.com. There is no account and no silent backend.
