Daniel Gueli Gypsy Jazz Orchestra

Daniel Gueli Gypsy Jazz Orchestra is an Amsterdam gypsy-jazz orchestra led by double bassist Daniel Gueli.

Players named for this desk so far: Daniel Gueli (double bass), Fabio Marziali (alto saxophone / reeds), and Jonn Reyna (piano; also written Jon Reyna). Public research did not turn up a full fixed bill for every chair. Other chairs remain TBC and are confirmed when you inquire.

Daniel Gueli

Double bass

Half Dutch, half Italian. Started on guitar at twelve, then electric bass in rock bands; after nursing study and travel in Africa he took up the double bass and studied at the Conservatorium van Amsterdam. Public collaborator credits include Nano Stern, Philippe Lemm, Alexander Beets, Sven Rozier, Jonn Reyna, Fabio Marziali, and Saskia Laroo (world tours). In gypsy jazz he plays with the Marcia Bamberg Swing Quartet and The Mozes Rosenberg Trio, and has shared stages with Stochelo, Jimmy, Mozes, and Nouche Rosenberg among others. Hosts a Monday Acoustic Session at Kaap & Tein, Amsterdam.

Fabio Marziali

Alto saxophone / reeds

Italian alto saxophonist; also flute, clarinet, and piano. Jazz master’s degree (9/10) at the Conservatorium van Amsterdam; classical saxophone degree (same mark) at Conservatorio G. Pergolesi, Fermo. Has worked resorts, hotels, and cruise lines; performances listed for Italy, the Netherlands, UAE, Thailand, USA, Spain, Kenya, Malta and more. Leader of the NY Quintet album Windows and Lights (2018). Named as a collaborator on Daniel Gueli’s public biography. No separate published Gypsy Jazz Orchestra bill listing every chair was found; chair here follows his public primary instrument.

JR Photo pending

Jonn Reyna

Piano

Dutch jazz pianist (international stage name of Jan Reinen; also written Jon Reyna). Studied at the Royal Conservatory in The Hague (Rob van Kreeveld, Rob van Bavel) and at the Conservatory in Hilversum with Henk Elkerbout, graduating with distinction. Style draws on boogie-woogie, bebop, blues, gospel, Latin, tango, and gypsy music among others. Leader of the Jonn Reyna Quartet; album Consolidaõ with Gene Jackson, Rick Margitza, Joris Teepe and guests. Named as a collaborator on Daniel Gueli’s public biography. Piano chair for this orchestra per the booking desk.
