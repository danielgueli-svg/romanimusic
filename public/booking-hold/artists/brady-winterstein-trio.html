Brady Winterstein Trio

Brady Winterstein is a lead guitarist from Forbach, in Lorraine, and a nephew of the guitarist Hono Winterstein. He took up the guitar at eleven. While still in his teens he was invited onto a Dorado Schmitt family record and onto Génération Django, a Dreyfus Jazz compilation. Early French notices also recall a France 3 Alsace report in which Biréli Lagrène gave him his blessing.

His first album, Happy Together, was recorded in 2011 for Plus Loin Music (PL 4542), with Hono Winterstein and double bassist Xavier Nikq. Brady was seventeen. The programme sits in jazz manouche and reaches outside it: the Turtles’ “Happy Together,” the Beatles’ “Lady Madonna,” Ellington’s “Solitude,” with guests including Marcel Loeffler, Thierry Maillard, Adrien Moignard, Martin Weiss, and Dominique Di Piazza.

Savoir-Vivre was released on 13 June 2013 under the Brady Winterstein Trio with Martin Weiss. The trio is based in Forbach. There is no separate official site; Bandcamp carries the record. The people around him change with the date. A 2015 Mulhouse bill listed Benji Winterstein and bassist Gino Roman; later Forbach concerts have put him back beside Hono. This desk confirms rhythm guitar and bass when you book, and does not treat an old sleeve as the current band.

Public bills already include Samois-sur-Seine, Jazz à Vienne with Dorado Schmitt, the Zénith in Strasbourg with Roberto Alagna, the Nuits Musicales d’Uzès, the Monte-Carlo Jazz Festival, and Djangofestivalen at Cosmopolite in Oslo. He is also on Hono Winterstein’s album Horizons (2019). Concerts, festivals, and private events.
