Gypsy jazz · vocals · formed 2018

Marcia Bamberg Swing Quintet

Marcia Bamberg sings French and English gypsy-jazz songs with John Ligthart and Ronald Weel on guitar and Daniel Gueli on double bass. The quintet formed in 2018. Marcia, Ronald, and Daniel had already played together for years when they met Ligthart. A visit to the Django Reinhardt Festival in Fontainebleau, among Sinti players, is what started this group.

Jazz manouche grew in the 1930s among Romani and Sinti musicians in Paris: musette, American swing, and the string-band sound of Django Reinhardt and Stéphane Grappelli. A lot of that repertoire is instrumental. This quintet puts the voice back in, and Marcia talks the evening — the songs, a little of the history — so the room is not sitting through a lecture.

The five

Marcia Bamberg — voice, and the spoken thread of the night.

John Ligthart — solo guitar. Has played with Koen De Cauter (Waso Quartet / Vaya Con Dios), Robin Nolan, and Jelle van Tongeren, among others.

Ronald Weel — rhythm guitar. Has played rhythm with Paulus Schäfer and Mozes Rosenberg.

Daniel Gueli — double bass.

Christian van Heemert — violin.

What is already public

The quintet’s own notes list concerts in Germany, Portugal, England, and France, including the Django Reinhardt Festival at Samois-sur-Seine / Fontainebleau, Festival Django Portugal, and rooms in the Netherlands such as Amersfoort Jazz and Zaantheater. Guests named in those notes include Paulus Schäfer, Mozes Rosenberg, Gismo Graf, Tim Kliphuis, and Christian van Heemert. On 11 November 2023 they sang “La Vie en Rose” on Dutch television, SBS6 Ranking the Talent.
