Jazz manouche · trio

Brady Winterstein Trio

Lineup Brady Winterstein, lead guitar; Nucky Basile, rhythm guitar; Daniel Gueli, double bass.

Brady Winterstein is a gypsy-jazz guitarist from Forbach, and the nephew of Hono Winterstein. In 2011, at 17, he made his first album, Happy Together, on Plus Loin Music, with Hono on rhythm guitar. The record keeps the manouche swing and brings in pop songs from the 1970s. He has played the Django Reinhardt Festival in Samois-sur-Seine, Jazz à Vienne, and Djangofestivalen at Cosmopolite in Oslo. He has played the Django Reinhardt Festival in Saint-Marcellin, and in Fontainebleau, where that festival is held now.

Jazz manouche · trio

Django Rosenberg Trio

Lineup Django Rosenberg, lead guitar; Noekie Basily, rhythm guitar; Daniel Gueli, double bass.

Django Rosenberg is a Sinti gypsy-jazz guitarist of the Rosenberg family. He leads a trio with Noekie Basily on rhythm guitar and Daniel Gueli on double bass. They played the Gypsy Festival in Tilburg on 22 August 2026.

Swing · vocals · quartet

Marcia Bamberg Swing Quartet / Quintet

Lineup

  • Marcia Bamberg, vocals
  • John Ligthart, solo guitar
  • Ronald Weel, rhythm guitar
  • Daniel Gueli, double bass
  • Christiaan van Hemert, violin

French and English swing songs, with the voice in the set, for concerts, festivals, and private concerts since 2018.

The group can also be booked as a quintet with Christiaan van Hemert on violin. He plays as a guest with the Rosenberg Trio, and he made the La Pompe Live app.
