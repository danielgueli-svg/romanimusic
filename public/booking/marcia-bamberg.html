Marcia Bamberg Swing Quartet / Quintet

Lineup

  • Marcia Bamberg, vocals
  • John Ligthart, solo guitar
  • Ronald Weel, rhythm guitar
  • Daniel Gueli, double bass
  • Christiaan van Hemert, violin

French and English swing songs, with the voice in the set, for concerts, festivals, and private concerts since 2018.

The group can also be booked as a quintet with Christiaan van Hemert on violin. He plays as a guest with the Rosenberg Trio, and he made the La Pompe Live app.
